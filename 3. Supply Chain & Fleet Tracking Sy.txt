3. Supply Chain & Fleet Tracking System

Focuses on state machines, complex queries, and logistical data relationships.
V1 (Fleet & Roles): Roles (Dispatcher, Driver, Manager). Create Vehicles, Drivers,
and Warehouses tables with foreign key relationships dictating which driver operates
which vehicle.

V2 (Shipment Lifecycle): Create Shipments and Routes. A shipment must move
through strict states (Processing -> Dispatched -> In Transit -> Delivered).

V3 (Multi-Stop Assignments): Implement many-to-many relationships where a Route
contains multiple Shipments. Drivers can log in and view their ordered stop-list for
the day

V4 (Proof of Delivery): Add media uploads. Drivers must upload a photo of the
delivered package and a digital signature before the system allows the shipment
state to change to "Delivered."

V5 (Bulk Route Processing): Dispatchers can upload a daily CSV manifest of orders.
The backend parses the CSV, automatically creates shipments, and flags
unassigned routes.
